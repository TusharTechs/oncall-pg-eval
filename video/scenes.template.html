08Two honest footnotes
Ticket H5 · "repair the data, then enforce"
__H5_FRONTIER__
frontier runs passed. When the ticket names the repair, models do it first and avoid the trap.
Pilot finding · GPT-6.1 Sol retry loop
175 ms lock wait, 10 ms pause → an exclusive lock request sits in the queue 95% of the time → app stalls 0.5–2.8 s (6/6 runs).
Ablation: pg_sleep(0.01→0.2) → PASS 236 ms
On the final prompt Sol wrote a safer loop (C1 3/3 pass) → reported as a hazard, not a headline.
09The lesson
Frontier models have memorized the safe-migration playbook.
They miss what it doesn't spell out:
a NOT VALID constraint that still firesa retry loop polite to the DB, not the app
You only find those by running the plan against a live system.
